## Standing lessons

- 2026-10-25: Keep army parity with the neighbours. On Deity the AI out-builds and out-researches you, so an army that falls behind invites an early attack.
- 2026-10-25: Build walls in border cities before the first war, not after.
- 2026-10-25: Let the AI attack into you. Defending behind walls and good terrain is cheaper than attacking out of the gate; counter-attack when its army is spent.
- 2026-10-25: Every 10 turns, check the religion, culture and diplomatic victory progress of every rival (`get_victory_progress`). Deity AIs win by these routes quietly.
